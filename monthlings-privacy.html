Privacy Policy: Monthlings Poster

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What the app does

Monthlings Poster is a private publishing tool. It posts the Monthlings brand's own Reels to its own Instagram account and Facebook Page through the Meta Graph API.

Data

The app uses only access tokens for the Monthlings Instagram account and Facebook Page, and only to publish their own content and read their own post statistics. It collects no data from viewers, followers or other users. It never sells, shares or uses data for advertising.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Data deletion

The Page owner can remove the app at any time in Facebook Settings, under Business Integrations; the stored tokens are then deleted. To ask for deletion, message the Monthlings Instagram account.
