About Pod Clips Lab

How the channel works

Pod Clips Lab is a small clip channel. We take long-form podcast and interview episodes and cut them into short vertical clips of 20 to 60 seconds, each built around one complete story.

Sources and rights

We only clip shows whose owners invite clippers through official clipping campaigns, or that give permission directly. When a clip is part of a paid campaign, it carries an #ad label and the platform's paid-partnership disclosure.

Our process

  1. We transcribe new episodes and find moments that have a clear setup and payoff.
  2. Each candidate is checked for accuracy, context and quality before it is edited.
  3. We frame the speaker for vertical video and add word-by-word captions.
  4. We publish the finished clip to our own accounts.

Pod Clips Lab Publisher

Pod Clips Lab Publisher is our private posting tool. It uploads our own finished clips to our own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. Nobody outside the channel uses it, and it collects no data from viewers. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
